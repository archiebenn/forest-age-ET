## 22nd March  
###  paper 1  
**Bridge to the future: Important lessons from 20 years of  ecosystem observations made by the OzFlux network**  

#### overview 

#### notes  
- ecosystems at ozflux show return to carbon sinks upon return of favourable conditions  
- ozflux data have potential for reducing uncertainties in remote sensing products  
- remote sensing products provide opportunities to develop new theories and models  
- Australian ecosystems span extreme climate gradients (hottest, most variable rainfall in FLUXNET) making them a natural lab for understanding climate extremes  
- droughts and heatwaves can flip ecosystems from co2 sinks to sources  
-  Southern hemisphere and extreme-climate sites are unerrepresented in global products  

### paper 2  
** How eddy covariance flux measurements have contributed to  our understanding of Global Change Biology **

#### notes  
- carbon and water fluxes respond non-linearly to changes in light/temp/soil moisture/co2 and need long term data to make responses clear  
- extreme events have disproportionate effects on annual carbon budgets  
- ML + satellite remote sensing + flux tower data = standard approach to upscaling eg FLUXCOM  
- high interannual variability means long term trends in NEE are hard to detect  
- water use efficiency increasing ~2% per year across temperate/boreal forests likely from increasing co2
